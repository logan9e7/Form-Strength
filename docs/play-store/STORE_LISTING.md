# Form & Strength — proposed Play listing

Status: draft for the 1.7.1 internal-test candidate; not submitted.

App name: Form & Strength

Short description (under 80 characters):
Track meals, workouts, water and habits with an offline fitness diary.

Full description:

Build a daily routine with Form & Strength. Keep your food diary, workouts, water and habits together on your Android phone.

FOOD AND NUTRITION
Type common foods and portions to estimate calories. Review and edit the result before saving. Save product-label portions and repeat meals from My foods. Protein totals show when values are incomplete. Recipes, cooking oil and portions affect estimates.

MOVEMENT AND ROUTINES
Log activities, follow guided strength sessions and record active and rest time separately. Track gym, mobility, protein-shake and no-added-sugar habits. Choose optional daytime reminders controlled by Android notification and battery settings.

YOUR PROGRESS
Record weight and optional measurements, review recent history, and set your own water and protein goals. A blank day is shown as missing information rather than a completed zero-intake day.

OPTIONAL HEALTH CONNECT
On Android 14 and newer, grant read access to steps, distance, active calories, total calories and sleep-session duration. Your band or companion app must supply supported data to Health Connect. Device readings are distinguished from manual estimates. There is no universal direct band connection.

OPTIONAL GEMINI ESTIMATES
Use your own Google AI Studio API key to request a meal estimate. Each text or photo request requires your approval before it is sent to Google's Gemini API. Review estimates before saving. Internet access and an eligible Google API project are required; quotas and charges depend on that project. No key or paid AI subscription is included.

LOCAL RECORDS
Core logging works offline. No app account, ads or analytics. Records stay in app-private storage; optional approved Gemini requests leave the device. Export a backup before changing phones. Uninstalling or clearing app storage deletes local records; exported files remain where you saved them.

Form & Strength provides general fitness information and estimates. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prescribe a diet. Ask a qualified professional about individual restrictions.
