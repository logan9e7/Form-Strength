Form & Strength privacy policy

Candidate policy • 26 November 2026 • App package com.logan.formstrength

Form & Strength is an offline fitness diary maintained by the Form & Strength project. This policy describes version 1.7.1. Core logging requires no app account. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and has no publisher-operated data backend.

Records on your phone

Optional profile details, measurements, goals, food and activity entries, water, habits, routines, onboarding choices and serving prices are stored in the app's private SQLite database. Reminder choices and settings are stored locally. App-private storage is protected by Android's application sandbox; we do not claim the SQLite database is separately encrypted. Automatic Android backup is disabled.

Health Connect

On Android 14 and newer you can grant read access to active calories burned, total calories burned, steps, distance and sleep-session duration. These support your daily activity and progress views. The app reads in the foreground and caches aggregated readings with dates, time zones, sources and freshness information in private local storage. These readings are not sent to Gemini, a publisher server or advertisers, and are excluded from JSON backups. No health writes, heart rate, routes, background-read or extended-history permission is requested.

Revoke access in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions. On the next access check, revoked metrics are cleared from the local cache. You can also clear cached readings from the app's Device controls. Your independent manual diary remains separate.

Optional Google Gemini requests

You can configure your own Google AI Studio API key. It is stored encrypted using an Android Keystore key in app-private preferences and excluded from JSON backups. The API key authenticates HTTPS requests to Google's Gemini API. It is not embedded by the publisher and is not exposed to the web interface.

Before each request, a native confirmation asks you to approve the meal text or meal photo being sent to Google, together with nutrition-estimation instructions. No profile, saved diary or Health Connect readings are included. Photos are resized and re-encoded as JPEG without original EXIF/location metadata, but faces, text, documents and other private details visible in the image remain visible. Avoid including such information. The app never saves an AI estimate as a diary meal without your review and save action.

Google processes approved inputs under its Gemini API terms, usage policies and your project settings. Processing, retention, human review or use to improve services may depend on your project and service tier; do not assume an unpaid project has the same handling as paid services. Check Google's current terms before sending personal information. Local deletion does not delete content Google has already processed. API costs and quotas belong to your project.

Camera and reminders

The app delegates capture to an installed camera app using a temporary permission for one private capture URI. Original captures are removed after preparation or cancellation; abandoned captures are pruned after 24 hours when cleanup next runs. Prepared image data is temporary. Camera apps are separate services with their own policies. Optional inexact local reminders follow Android permission, notification-channel, DND and battery controls; the app requests no exact-alarm or DND-bypass access.

Backups, retention and deletion

Manual records remain locally until you delete individual supported entries, clear app storage or uninstall. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes the local database, settings and locally stored encrypted API-key material. Remove your configured Gemini key through Connections if you only want to disconnect AI. No app account exists to delete.

You may export readable JSON backups to a destination you choose. Backups contain manual profile/diary/activity data and saved portions, but not Gemini keys or Health Connect cache. Other apps or cloud destinations you select handle their copies under their own policies. Delete exported copies separately; uninstalling does not delete them. Keep backups private.

External links and contact

External reference and support links open in your browser. Their operators handle access under their own policies. Privacy questions can be raised with the developer through Form & Strength support. This is a public issue tracker: do not post health records, photos, API keys or other private information. A public support email must be confirmed before the Play listing is submitted.

Fitness information and changes

Estimates are general fitness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. We will update this policy when app data handling changes and identify the policy date here.
