How calorie estimates work

Adult Compendium MET references use duration and body weight. Active calories subtract a resting-energy estimate; gross calories include it. Fitness, intensity, rest time and technique change actual burn.

Record active time for weights, excluding long rest periods. “30 min walking” assumes moderate pace on level ground. Typed speed, incline or unfamiliar activities need a matching reference; unsupported details are not silently ignored.

Manual activities can overlap each other or your band. Only manual activities explicitly marked as missing from device data are added to synced burn. Other activity logs remain separate. These figures are not a prescribed food allowance.

Reading windows & missing data

Permission off means access was denied. No data shared means access exists but the source has no record in this window. Zero is shown only when actually reported. Older history is subject to Health Connect’s access limits.

Connect your fitness band
  1. Open your band's companion app and sync the band.
  2. Enable Health Connect sharing there for calories, steps, distance and sleep, if supported.
  3. Connect this app above and choose which read permissions to grant.
  4. Tap Sync. Missing readings remain blank; check the source app and its sharing settings.

A paired Bluetooth band alone does not provide these records. Some apps share only steps or total burn. The app can only read data actually written to Health Connect. This is a Health Connect connection, not a direct Google Fit or band login.

Synced aggregates are cached privately on this phone with dates, source labels and timestamps. Revoked metrics are cleared from that cache. They are not sent to Gemini or exported in backups. Total burn includes resting energy; do not add it to active calories.

Simple food habits

Include a protein source with main meals: eggs, curd, dal, beans, tofu, paneer, fish or chicken. Add vegetables and fruit, and keep rice or roti in portions that suit your activity.

Small reductions in sugary drinks, frequent fried snacks and excess cooking oil can help. Avoid crash dieting or skipping meals to compensate.

Privacy & guidance

Your records stay in this app's private SQLite database. No app account, ads or analytics. Core logging works offline. Optional Gemini sends only meal text you approve to Google; Health Connect data is never sent to AI. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes your records. Automatic cloud backup is disabled.
